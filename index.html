ORBGRID — Legal & Support

ORBGRID is a chain-reaction strategy puzzle game for Android and iOS. This site holds its privacy policy, terms of service, and player support information.

ORBGRID has no user accounts. You never sign up, log in, or give us your name or email address in order to play.

Documents

  • Privacy Policy — what is stored on your device, and which third parties process data.
  • Terms of Service — the rules for using the game and the one-time ORBGRIDplus purchase.
  • Support — how to get help, restore a purchase, or delete your data.

The game at a glance

  • A puzzle game with three ways to play: Solo vs CPU, Local Multiplayer on one device, and a daily puzzle.
  • Playable offline. Progress and settings stay on your device.
  • Free to play, funded by advertising.
  • An optional one-time purchase, ORBGRIDplus, removes advertising permanently. It is not a subscription.
